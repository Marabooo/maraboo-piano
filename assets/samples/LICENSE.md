**Salamander Grand Piano V3** by Alexander Holm — licensed under **Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 (CC BY 3.0)**,
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/

Downloaded on 24 Nov 2026 from the copy hosted by the Tone.js project: `https://tonejs.github.io/audio/salamander/`
(files `Fs2, A2, C3, Ds3, Fs3, A3, C4, Ds4, Fs4, A4, C5, Ds5, Fs5, A5, C6, Ds6, Fs6 .mp3`).

**Changes made (as CC BY requires us to say):** each file was trimmed to its first 6 seconds with leading
silence removed and a 1.8 s fade-out, mixed down to mono, and re-encoded as MP3 (LAME VBR quality 2, 44.1 kHz)
with ffmpeg. Total size ≈ 500 KB. Notes between samples are pitch-shifted in the browser (≤ 1.5 semitones).

Attribution is shown in the app under Settings ▸ About.
